Independent Space Systems Research

Spacecraft, propulsion, and future space infrastructure.

Tran Space Systems Research Lab is an independent research effort focused on spacecraft concepts, advanced propulsion, atmospheric entry and landing, and orbital infrastructure.

Research Areas

Spacecraft Concepts

Preliminary vehicle architectures, mission concepts, configuration studies, and future spacecraft design.

Advanced Propulsion

Physics-informed propulsion concepts, engine studies, and theoretical propulsion architecture.

Entry, Descent, and Landing

Atmospheric entry, thermal protection, descent systems, landing concepts, and planetary operations.

Orbital Infrastructure

Concepts for stations, habitats, assembly systems, offworld construction, and long-duration space architecture.
